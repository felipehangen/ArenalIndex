## Current public experience and accepted design decisions

Felipe wants a restrained, readable property explorer inspired by Airbnb, with little interface clutter.

- Desktop: listing grid and independent map side by side from 960 px; wider layouts can show three card columns.
- Intermediate screens: two-column listing layout from 600 px before the full desktop split.
- Mobile: scrollable list over the map, with navigation at the bottom.
- All card sizes show **one cover photo**, then a carousel. The old mobile three-photo collage was removed. Preserve photo selection across responsive changes.
- Listing details use the same presentation for buyer previews.
- Logo/header clipping and accidental property activation while scrolling were addressed.
- On the property detail page, the noninteractive location map allows vertical page scrolling on iPhone.
- Loading placeholders reserve card/map space. Map and catalog loading/failure have separate feedback and retries.
- Cards show title, locality, area, price and up to three compact feature icons/labels. Freeform prose stays off the result cards.
- Features derive from structured data; do not invent lake access or specific watercourse types from ambiguous fields.
- Filter icons are compact; “Condominio privado” distinguishes it from an ordinary residential development. Construction filters separate land without buildings from properties with buildings.
- “Explorar” means browsing, not a misleading search entry point; neighborhood selection remains in filters.
- Explorar/Guardados show active selection on desktop and mobile. Re-tapping the active section gives subtle feedback without resetting the view.
- Guardados uses a full mobile list; the map must not peek through.
- Sort opens a custom dialog outside the scrollable list. Opening/canceling/current-choice selection preserves position; a new sort starts at the first result while keeping the panel raised.
- Traditional sort icon: three descending horizontal bars.
- Default `explore` sorting varies by **tab-session seed**, stable across reloads/returns in that session. It does not alter filters or map membership.
- Other sorts: total price ascending/descending, price per land m² ascending, land area descending.
- Menu puts buyer options first, then language/contact, then “Para publicar” with publishing/account options.
- “Publicar propiedad” opens a contact panel, not unrestricted sign-up.
- WhatsApp/email links prepare a message; the visitor sends it.

## Currency and language

**Currency**
- Seller enters one original asking price and currency, USD or CRC.
- USD is the default viewing currency; CRC is another display currency, not a filter excluding USD listings.
- `site/pricing.js` prepares converted values once per catalog load. Cards, map, histograms, sorting, total-price filters and price-per-m² filters share those values.
- Price per m² divides by **land area**, never construction area.
- Keep original price/currency for contact messages and the listing's asking-price display.
- No “≈” symbol. Equivalent is labeled discreetly; rate/source/date are under the filters' “Tipo de cambio” disclosure. No currency badge at the top of the menu.
- BCCR public purchase/sale feeds are averaged as an indicative rate. `scripts/update-exchange-rate.mjs` validates a common effective date and preserves the last valid snapshot on failure.
- GitHub Actions runs at 06:17 Costa Rica and retries at 09:17, and also on pushes. A successful same-day rate is reused.

## Accounts, editing, review, and publication

Neutral customer-facing language: **Mi cuenta / Mis anuncios**, since users may be owners, brokers or representatives.

- `/account/` redirects to `/cuenta/`; old `/owner/` also redirects. Internal “owner” module/API names are retained for compatibility.
- Admin creates/activates accounts. Reusable invitation codes were removed.
- Google login works for administrator-authorized emails.
- Alternative: email plus an admin-created temporary password, requiring replacement before using listings.
- Admin access itself is Google plus a server-side allowlist; account passwords never grant admin access.
- Unauthorized Google sign-in should show an error and a retry path; do not let the button silently disappear.
- **Bare /newlisting/ requires account sign-in. Anonymous creation is rejected by production intake.**
- “Agregar la ficha de este navegador” was removed.
- Draft save is available at the top, including incomplete drafts. Local autosave does not substitute for saving to the account for another device.
- General “Guardar Copia”, redundant prepared-text preview controls, and the old confusing back link were removed/reworked.
- Required-field indicators use light red; review lists exactly what is missing and links back to the relevant fields/photo groups.
- Photo checks require correct categories/tags, not merely uploaded files: land photos, cover, entry/approach, exterior per building, or an accepted exception where allowed.
- “Venta y contacto” and “Resumen y envío” are separate.
- “Ver como comprador” previews the public presentation without publishing or exposing private documents.
- Locality is suggested from the entry map pin via Nominatim/OSM, with caching/rate limiting; the administrator can correct it.
- Clicking a **real** admin listing opens its editor. Save/preview/next review or publication action are grouped; secondary actions are under “Más acciones”.
- Demo entries use the managed demo editor/actions. Their editing fields are narrower than the full intake schema.
- Applicable row actions include edit, approval/publication, unlist and delete.
- Deletion requires typing **DELETE or BORRAR**, checked in UI and API.
- Complex “awaiting changes” feedback was simplified: reopen as draft, coordinate corrections by call/WhatsApp. Legacy `changes_requested` records display as editable drafts; do not reintroduce a mandatory per-field commenting workflow.

**Updating an already published real listing**
1. Open/edit and save changes.
2. Prepare/send the new version for review.
3. Review and approve the version.
4. Choose **Actualizar publicación**.

The previous public snapshot remains visible while edits are a draft. Saving or approving alone does not replace it. Explicitly unlisting hides it. Accounts can prepare/reopen/unlist their own listings; only admin can approve/publish. Concurrency checks tie actions to the current version.

Private documents, contact fields, tokens and internal comments must stay out of public projections. Public photos are verified against the current publication before receiving temporary signed URLs. Deleting a record makes it inaccessible and removes its publication; it does not physically purge every media object.
